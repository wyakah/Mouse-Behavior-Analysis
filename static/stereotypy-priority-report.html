What the evidence supports

  • CBAS contributes explicit labels for all three priority behaviors. Its overhead camera differs from your side view. LabGym contributes side-view grooming and rearing examples; its mixed “foraging” label is not treated as digging.
  • Normalization and PCA fit training data only. Unknown labels stay unknown. Training/test source groups stay separate. LabGym clips are training-only because parent recording identities are unavailable.
  • CBAS test sampling covers complete released files at 2 Hz. MIT uses four frames per previously selected stable two-second window. These are different evaluation tasks and should not be compared as a single improvement percentage.
  • No local behavior accuracy has been measured. The supplied mice are development recordings, including prior unlabeled adaptation. An independent local validation set must include positive events and confounders; duration and bout errors need source-frame review.
  • Nonfood gnawing, jumping and circling retain their earlier weak-supervision status. Future 20-minute experimental recordings have not yet been scored.
